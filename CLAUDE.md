# 30K-4-All

BattleScribe/New Recruit data files for Horus Heresy (Panoptica/Liber Antiquia ruleset).

## Reference rulebooks

Source-of-truth PDFs for unit rules, points, and wargear are in `reference/`.
These are downloaded from https://hh-ageofdarkness.itch.io/panoptica-2e-content
and are not this project's content — do not commit them (already covered by
`.gitignore`'s default-ignore rule).

- `reference/Liber-Antiquia_PAGES_v1.6.pdf` — Saedath and other Antiquia factions
- `reference/Liber-Centura_PAGES_v1.6.pdf`
- `reference/Liber-Imperatus_PAGES_v1.2.pdf`
- `reference/Liber-Ingenium_PAGES_v1.3.pdf`
- `reference/Liber-Panoptica_PAGES_v5.2.pdf`
- `reference/Liber-Panoptica_v5.2_LI.pdf`

These PDFs are large — read specific page ranges rather than the whole file.
